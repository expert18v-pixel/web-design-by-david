Questions

Before you ask

How much does a website cost? +

It depends on the number of pages and features you need — a one-page site and a full online store are very different jobs. Send a request with what you need and I’ll give you a straight quote. You see a demo of your website before we finalise anything about payment.

How long does it take? +

Once I have your answers and content, most standard business websites get their first demo within a few days. Bigger sites with stores or booking systems take longer — I’ll give you a realistic timeline with your quote.

What do you need from me? +

Just the basics: your business name, what you offer, your contact details, and any photos or a logo you already have. No photos yet? We can start with the structure and add visuals as we go — you don’t need any technical knowledge.

I already have a website. Can you fix it? +

Yes — redesigns are part of what I do. Pick “Redesign of my current site” in the request form and include your current site’s address in the details, and I’ll review it before quoting.
